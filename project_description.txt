Steel Temperature Prediction for Energy Optimization

Developed a machine learning solution to predict final molten steel temperature during ladle treatment at a metallurgical plant. The project addresses a critical challenge in secondary steelmaking, where electricity for arc heating accounts for 25-35% of production costs.
Based on historical data from the steel melting process I built and tuned a CatBoost Regressor achieving 6.0°C test MAE, that
demonstrated potential for 66% temperature error reduction compared to rule-based heating approaches with estimated cost savings of €487.8K/year through optimized heating strategies.

Tech stack: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, CatBoost, Optuna, PyOD
Approach: Comprehensive EDA, data preprocessing, feature engineering (power factor, apparent power, work calculations), multicollinearity analysis, model comparison (Ridge Regression vs. CatBoost with Optuna tuning)
