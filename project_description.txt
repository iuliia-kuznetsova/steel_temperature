Steel Temperature Prediction for Energy Optimization

Developed a data science solution to predict final molten steel temperature during ladle treatment at a metallurgical plant. 
The project addresses a critical challenge in secondary steelmaking, where electricity for arc heating accounts for 25-35% of production costs.

Based on historical data from the steel melting process I've built and tuned a CatBoost Regressor achieving 6.0°C test MAE, that
demonstrated potential for 66% temperature error reduction compared to rule-based heating approaches with estimated cost savings of €487.8K/year through optimized heating strategies.

Tech stack: Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, CatBoost, Optuna, PyOD
Approach: 
- exploratory data analysis, 
- data preprocessing, 
- feature engineering (power factor, apparent power, work calculations), 
- multicollinearity analysis, 
- modelling with Ridge Regression and CatBoostRegressor with Optuna hyperparameters optimization,
- model comparison,
- conclusion on the best model performance quality.
